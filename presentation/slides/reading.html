Two books and two sites. Liz Rice's Learning eBPF is short and current and explains the mechanics we covered today, including the verifier, maps and CO-RE. Brendan Gregg's BPF Performance Tools is the reference for what to measure and which tool answers which question. His eBPF page at brendangregg.com/ebpf.html collects the tools, talks and diagrams, and ebpf.io is the community hub with docs and a project list. The examples are in the repository and self-testing on Rocky 9: run them on a staging box this week. Then questions.
