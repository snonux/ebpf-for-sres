Further reading

Two books and a few links

BPF Performance Tools

Brendan Gregg, Addison-Wesley 2019. The tool-by-tool reference: 150+ tools, the methodology behind them, bpftrace and bcc.

Learning eBPF

Liz Rice, O'Reilly 2023. Short and current: how programs, maps, the verifier, CO-RE and libbpf fit together.

ebpf.io · docs.kernel.org/bpf · bpftrace.org · nakryiko.com (CO-RE and libbpf) · github.com/aquasecurity/libbpfgo · github.com/snonux/ior

Two books. Brendan Gregg's BPF Performance Tools is the reference for what to measure and which tool answers which question; it predates CO-RE becoming mainstream but the methodology has not aged. Liz Rice's Learning eBPF is shorter and newer and explains the mechanics we covered today, including CO-RE. Andrii Nakryiko's blog is the best source on CO-RE and libbpf.
